# Top 100 English words → draft cognitoforms

**Status:** provisional analysis, not canonical vocabulary.

Source: [NGSL Headwords and Definitions by Frequency](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/evan-007/ngsl-dictionary/master/NGSL-Headwords-and-Definitions-byFreq.csv)

This pass deliberately separates **semantic derivation** from **current geometric feasibility**. I did not add semes merely to make a shape fit the present layout. If the definition points to a seme combination that the current ring arrangement cannot connect, the entry is marked **layout blocked**. That is useful evidence for rearranging the 24 semes later.

Legend:

- **clean** — the current seme inventory captures the definition well.
- **conventional** — the semantic core works, but the exact English lexical distinction would need lexical convention/context.
- **inventory-gap** — the definition relies on a distinction not presently represented by the 24 semes.
- **polysemy** — the English definition merges multiple senses that should probably not share one cognitoform.
- **blocked-current-layout** — at least one intended conceptual bond cannot be realized directly under the current coordinate map, or multiple radial bonds demand incompatible ring alignments.

## First structural findings

1. **English function words collapse heavily.** OF, AND, WITH, HAVE, and ALSO all place strong pressure on **WITH** rather than demanding five separate primitives.
2. **Deixis and discourse roles are the biggest inventory gap.** Speaker, addressee, male/female, proximal/distal, and source/goal direction repeatedly appear but are not independently expressible.
3. **Interrogatives expose another gap.** WHAT, WHO, WHICH, WHEN, and WHERE naturally want an “unknown/requested information” operator. NEGATION + KNOW is a plausible semantic analysis, but the current layout does not permit that bond.
4. **The current geometry blocks several high-value compounds.** Examples include WITH—PLACE (ON/BY), DO—SIGN (SAY), CHANGE—WITH (GET), MANY—ALIVE (PEOPLE), and KNOW—EXIST inside the truth sense of RIGHT.
5. **Continuous ring rotation does not solve ring-depth skips.** It can align any seme on adjacent rings, but it cannot directly connect R1↔R3, R1↔R4, or R2↔R4 semes.
6. **Some lexical distinctions probably should remain conventional rather than primitive.** YEAR vs DAY, TO vs FROM, GO vs COME, THIS vs THAT, and AFTER vs its opposite all share broad semantic cores but need polarity, scale, viewpoint, or convention to distinguish them.

## Recommended use

Treat this file as a **pressure map** for the next design pass. Before canonizing all 100 cognitoforms, use the intended bond graph to optimize seme placement and separately decide whether recurring inventory gaps deserve new structural mechanisms (for example deixis/discourse role or interrogativity) rather than new semes.
