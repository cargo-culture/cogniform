What changed

The four structural mechanisms

?

QUERY

Marks a seme or subgraph as the requested unknown. WHAT, WHO, WHERE and WHEN no longer require fake NEGATION/KNOW machinery.

@

INDEX

Anchors a concept to discourse origin, addressee, or an established referent, with optional near/far deixis.

±

ORDER polarity

Gives ORDER a positive or negative orientation along PLACE, TIME, SIZE, or another understood dimension.

#

MEASURE

Attaches quantitative data to TIME or SIZE instead of inventing primitive semes for every conventional unit.

Why routed bonds fix the geometry

A semantic bond is distinct from the path used to draw it. Every physical segment still follows the existing ring/spoke lattice. Intermediate seme positions may lie under the route, but they remain completely inactive and unmarked. A seme contributes meaning only when its node is explicitly active. Direct bonds remain preferred because they are shorter.

What approval would mean

Approval would authorize implementing these mechanics in the main editor and rerunning the 100-word corpus with the revised rules. It would not freeze the current seme coordinates or the exact visual styling of the operator marks.
